# Quick start for students

1. **Scan your card.** The QR code on your card opens your own page. Keep your card safe and do not share it.
2. **Add a goal.** Tap **New goal** → choose what you want to get better at → choose a goal or write **My own words** → **How will you practise?** → **Save my goal**.
3. **Say how it is going.** Tap **How is it going?** → choose your goal → tap a face from 1 to 5, or write **Something I learned** or a **Note to myself** → **Save**.
4. **Show what you did.** Tap **Show my work** → write something, add a link or a document → **Save my work**.
5. **Make your avatar.** Tap **My avatar** → **Change my avatar** → pick → **Save my avatar**.

Not sure what to write? Tap a sentence under **Start with:** and finish it with your own words.

When you finish a goal, open **My goals** and press **I did it!** **Back** takes you back one step.

Lost your card? Tell your teacher. They will give you a new one.

More help, and a part for parents: [Students and parents guide](student.md).
