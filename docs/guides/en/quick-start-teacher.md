# Quick start for teachers

Your first lesson with Class Compass in five steps. The full [Teacher guide](teacher.md) explains everything in detail.

1. **Sign in.** Open the invite link from your administrator and choose a password (at least 10 characters) or, if the school has set up email, email sign-in links.
2. **Set your PIN.** Teacher menu → **Profile and PIN** → **Set your PIN**: 4 to 8 digits. Without a PIN you cannot open the classroom screen. See [PIN and classroom lock](teacher.md#pin-and-classroom-lock).
3. **Check your class.** **Class settings**: choose the class language and switch on the modules you want. Add your students with **Students** → **Import a student list** if your administrator has not done it.
4. **Teach.** **Open the classroom screen**, check the class at the top (for example **3-A · English**), press **Start lesson**, go through the steps and press **Begin lesson**. Use the routine, the timer, groups and badges during the lesson. **Teacher area** asks for your PIN, so the class never sees your pages on the projector.
5. **End the lesson.** **End lesson** → reveal the secret students, thank the assistant, add your reflection → **Save and end lesson**.

Afterwards, in the teacher area:

- **Student links**: print the students' QR cards (**Print all cards**). Keep the links private.
- **Homework**, **Goals**, **Achievements** and **Reports** for the work between lessons.
- **Help** in the teacher menu opens the guides at any time.
