# Privacy

This page says what Class Compass keeps about the people who use it, and what happens when you sign in. It is public: you can read it without an account.

Class Compass is classroom software that a school runs for its own teachers and students. **The school is responsible for the data** (in Turkey: under KVKK). The site holds only what the school's staff and students put into it.

## What the site keeps

- **Staff accounts** (teachers and administrators): name, username, email address, interface language, and a record of sign-ins and of changes made with the account.
- **Students**: name, class, and a student number if the school enters one; what happens in lessons (attendance, homework status, counters, groups, classroom roles); and what the student adds to their own page (goals, reflections, work they attach, their avatar).
- **What teachers record about a student**: feedback on goals and work, achievements, Super Speakers points, the levels a teacher sets for making groups, and private notes. Levels and private notes never appear on the classroom screen or on the student's page. In the site, a private note is seen only by the teacher who wrote it; among teachers, levels are seen only by the teacher of that subject in that class. An administrator's **Audit log** shows that a level was changed, by whom and when, but not the level itself. Levels and private notes are in the school's full data export, which an administrator makes on purpose; the export of one student's data contains the levels but not the private notes. When the site stops a word that is not suitable for school in what a student types, it counts the attempt; the blocked text itself is not kept.
- **Files**: pictures, voice recordings and videos only when the school has switched them on after getting consent; documents a student or teacher attaches.
- **A log of changes** to shared records, so the school can see who changed what.

The site keeps no grades from other systems, no home addresses, no phone numbers and no payment details.

## Where it is kept and who can see it

- Everything is stored on the school's own Class Compass server and in the school's backups. It is not sold and not used for advertising.
- A teacher sees the classes and subjects they teach. An administrator of the school sees the school's data. A student sees their own page. Other students and other parents do not see a student's page.
- On the classroom screen the class sees names, avatars and positive things only.
- Emails (invites, sign-in links, password resets, a teacher's daily summary) are sent through the school's mail server.
- If the school shows the weather in the classroom, the weather service receives the school's map coordinates and nothing else.
- AI tools are off unless the school's administrator switches them on. The students and parents guide in the app's Help says what they can and cannot receive.

## Signing in

- **With a link or code sent by email**: the site sends an email to the address on your account. Nothing else leaves the site.
- **With a password**, where the school allows it: the password is stored only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back.
- **With a passkey**: your own device or password manager keeps the key and asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The site stores only the public half of the key, the name you gave it, when it was added and last used, and a few technical details about the key. The private key stays on your device or in your password manager. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device.
- **With Google**, where the school has set it up. You choose this yourself each time. Google then tells the site two things: **your email address** and **Google's own ID for your Google account**. The site uses them for one purpose: to find the account your school made for you and sign you in.
  - The site does not ask for, use or keep anything else from your Google account: no contacts, no mail, no files, no calendar, no profile picture.
  - The site keeps no Google access token and cannot act in your Google account.
  - Your email address and Google's ID are stored with your school account and are not shared with anyone. Google's ID is removed when the address on your account changes (by you in your profile or by the school) or the school deactivates the account.
  - Google learns that you signed in to this site and when. Google's own privacy policy applies to that.
  - A Google sign-in leaves your Google account open in the browser you used. Use it only on your own device.
- **With the school's own sign-in server**, where the school has connected one (a small icon on the sign-in page). That server tells the site your email address and its own ID for your account, which the site uses only to find your school account and sign you in. The site asks for nothing else and keeps no token from it. The ID is stored with your school account and removed when the address on it changes (by you or by the school) or the school deactivates the account. That server learns that you signed in to this site and when, and signing in this way leaves your account on that server open in the browser you used, so use it only on your own device.
- **Students** do not have accounts or passwords. A student opens their page with a link or card from the teacher; the link is the key, so keep it private.

## Cookies

The site sets a cookie that keeps you signed in, and short-lived cookies during sign-in. A teacher or administrator stays signed in on a device until they sign out, for 90 days without use, and for a year at most. It remembers a few choices on your device, such as the language. It uses no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies and loads nothing from other companies' servers.

## How long data is kept

The school decides. Deactivated accounts keep their history so that past records stay correct; an administrator can delete a student's records permanently once the student is deactivated. Backups made before a deletion keep the data until they expire.

## Your rights and who to ask

You can ask the school for a copy of the data about you or your child, for a correction, or for deletion (in Turkey: your rights under KVKK). The school's Class Compass administrator can export one student's data and delete it. **For any question about your data, contact the school that gave you your account or your child's link.**
