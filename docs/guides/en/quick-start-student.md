# Quick start for students

1. **Scan your card.** The QR code on your card opens your own page. Keep your card safe and do not share it.
2. **Add a goal.** **Add a goal** → choose what you want to get better at → **How will I practise?** → **Add my goal**.
3. **Write how it went.** Under **My reflections**, add a reflection, a note or **How I feel about it**.
4. **Show what you did.** Under **My evidence**, write something, add a link or a document.
5. **Make your avatar.** **Change my avatar** → pick → **Save my avatar**.

When you finish a goal, press **I did it!**

Lost your card? Tell your teacher. They will give you a new one.

More help, and a part for parents: [Students and parents guide](student.md).
