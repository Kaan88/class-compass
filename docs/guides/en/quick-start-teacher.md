# Quick start for teachers

Your first lesson with Class Compass in five steps. The full [Teacher guide](teacher.md) explains everything in detail.

1. **Sign in.** Open the invite link from your administrator and choose a password (at least 10 characters) or, if the school has set up email, email sign-in links.
2. **Set your PIN.** Teacher menu → **Profile and PIN** → **Set your PIN**: 4 to 8 digits. Without a PIN you cannot open the classroom screen. See [PIN and classroom lock](teacher.md#pin-and-classroom-lock).
3. **Check your class.** Teacher menu → **Class setup** → **Class settings**: choose the class language and switch on the modules you want. Add your students with **Import a student list** (same tab) if your administrator has not done it.
4. **Teach.** **Open the classroom screen**, check the class at the top (for example **3-A · English**), press **Start lesson**, go through the steps (tick what today's assistant has already done) and press **Begin lesson**. The routine and a big timer are at the top, the student cards in the middle and the **Lesson tools** buttons (attendance, assistant, groups, achievements…) at the bottom. If you hear nothing, tap **Sound is off · Turn sound on** in the top bar. **Teacher area** asks for your PIN, so the class never sees your pages on the projector.
5. **End the lesson.** **End lesson** → reveal the secret students, tick the assistant's duties and thank them, add your reflection → **Save and end lesson**.

Afterwards, in the teacher area:

- **Today** on the teacher menu: **Attendance**, **Homework**, **Goals**, **Reports** and the **Notifications** bell (what your students did on their own pages).
- **Class setup** → **Student links**: print the students' QR cards (**Print all cards**). Keep the links private.
- A student's name opens their statistics anywhere in the teacher area.
- Want the timer bigger or the counters in the bottom row? **Display** → **Customise layout** on the classroom screen ([Customise layout](teacher.md#customise-layout)).
- **Help** on the teacher menu opens the guides at any time.
